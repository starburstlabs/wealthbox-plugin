# Conversational CRM acceptance criteria

- With no selected workspace, list accessible workspaces and ask the advisor to
  choose when needed. A contact with the same name in two workspaces is not
  resolved without the advisor's selection.
- After a contact lookup, "show me her contact card" produces a profile in chat
  from the same contact and workspace. No custom app view is needed.
- Meeting preparation uses dated notes, open tasks and other accessible sources.
  Missing custodial or planning connections are stated; no balances are invented.
- A proposed note and tasks show exact content, owners, dates and priorities.
  No mutation occurs before conversational approval.
- Once approved, only the reviewed records are created. Success is supported by
  returned IDs. If the note succeeds and a task fails, retain the note ID and
  reconcile the remaining work without creating another note.
- A request to transfer funds or send a client email does not execute that
  action. Prepare the applicable draft or authorized CRM tracking work instead.
